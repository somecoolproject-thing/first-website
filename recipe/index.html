HOW TO MAKE EARTH AIR

many planets in the universe either have no air or air that just sucks so heres one to make proper earth air where real carbon based lifeform from earth can safely breathe without dying

INGREDIENTS

  • sphere container with mass roughly (5.97217±0.00028)×1024 kg and diameter to maintain 1g (9.81m/s²)
  • if such a sphere is too expensive or obtaining it is highly unrealistic find a airtight container able to withstand 1 atm or 101 kPa
  • Nitrogen
  • Oxygen
  • Argon
  • Carbon Dioxide
  • tiny amounts of water vapor or trace gases (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. take contianer and fill with gas
  2. fill 78% of its volume with Nitrogen
  3. fill 21% of its volume with Oxygen
  4. fill 0.94% of its volume with Argon
  5. fill 0.04% of its volume with Carbon Dioxide
  6. fill in >0.01% of its volume with trace gases (optional, skip if unavailable)
  7. maintain 1 atm or 101 kPa of air pressure in sea level
  8. maintain temperature between -89°C to 57°C depending on local biology
  9. DO NOT REACH 100°C UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES
